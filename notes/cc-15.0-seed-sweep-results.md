# cc-15.0 — seed sweep results (CSV-backed)

Generated 2026-09-10 by `scripts/export_rollout_results.py`, which writes:

- **`results/rollouts_all.csv`** — one row per rollout run (144 runs): domain, harness, arm,
  recipe, snapshot, seed, solved, n_tasks, pct, Wilson CI, `is_dup`, `is_smoke`, run_tag.
- **`results/rollouts_by_arm.csv`** — per (domain, arm, recipe, snapshot): n_runs, mean, sd,
  min/max, pooled rate, and the list of seeds.

Both are regenerable; nothing is hand-entered.

## Read the `recipe` column before pooling anything

Run tags alone conflate Stage-2 generations. Retail `thoughts_policy_adamw30` at seed 42
spans the SGD 1e-3 arm (~31%), the AdamW 1e-3 arm (~12%) and a 4-task smoke; averaging
them is meaningless. `recipe` is the checkpoint generation (`sgdlr1e_3`, `lr3e_3`,
`lr1e4`, `g8topK`, ...) and `is_smoke` flags runs with <=6 tasks.

`is_dup` marks snorkel same-seed reruns. The snorkel gyms are deterministic given a seed
(a same-seed "repeat" reproduced 40/40 tasks), so those carry no extra information and are
excluded from the per-arm summary. tau2 calls an unseeded external user simulator, so its
repeats are independent whether or not the seed changes.

## What the seed sweep changed

**1. The arm comparison is now properly powered on insurance** — every arm has 3-5
independent seeds instead of one sample:

| arm | n | mean | sd |
|---|---|---|---|
| `actions_only` (no thoughts at all) | 5 | **53.5%** | 2.5 |
| `thoughts_policy` | 3 | 51.7% | 3.1 |
| `thoughts_base` | 5 | 50.5% | 3.7 |
| `expert_thoughts_all` | 5 | 48.5% | 2.5 |

**Between-arm sd = 1.8 pt; within-arm (seed) sd = 3.0 pt.** The arm effect is smaller than
the noise from re-running one arm under a different seed. The nominal ordering even puts
`actions_only` first. There is no evidence that distilled thoughts help downstream --
inferred, base-scored, or human-written.

**2. tau2 confirms its own noise model rather than ranking arms.** Airline
`thoughts_policy` has sd **13.6** across three seeds (44.4 / 61.1 / 77.8); retail spans
21.4-31.0 within an arm. As predicted, 3 samples cannot resolve 5-pt differences there --
that needs ~51 runs (retail) and ~77 (airline). Airline's apparent `expert_thoughts_all`
lead (70.4%) sits inside that noise and should not be reported as a finding.

**3. `step_0020` > `step_best` holds everywhere it is measured**, and on insurance the gap
is enormous (48-54% vs 10-20%). Retail and airline show the same direction with much
smaller gaps, consistent with their noise floors.

## Caveats

- Several `step_best` cells are still n=1; only insurance `thoughts_policy` has n=3 there.
  The step_0020-vs-step_best contrast is nonetheless safe on insurance (~35 pt gap vs ~3 pt
  seed noise) and was independently confirmed at p=1.1e-12 over 160 pooled rollouts.
- Finance rows exist in the CSVs but are uninformative (floor effect: 9 of 10 fair-set
  rollouts ever run scored 0/10). Excluded from the table above.
- Airline n=18 per run; retail 42; insurance 40. CIs in `rollouts_all.csv` are Wilson and
  model only task-level sampling -- they understate tau2's true run-to-run variance.
