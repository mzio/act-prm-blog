# cc-13.0 — SGD `sft_flat` Stage-2 across airline / finance / insurance

Started 2026-09-02. **Status: PLANNED — queued behind the cc-12.0 chain.**

Follows [cc-12.0](cc-12.0-sgd-reversion.md), which established on retail that the
AdamW Stage-2 collapse is a property of the **fit regime**, not of Stage-2 SFT itself.

## Why this experiment exists

Retail, SGD 1e-3 `sft_flat`, 42 held-out tau2 tasks (base = 14.3%):

| arm / snapshot | completion |
|---|---|
| `thoughts_policy` / `step_0020` | **31.0%** (13/42) |
| `thoughts_policy` / `step_best` | 23.8% (10/42) |
| `actions_only` / `step_0020` | 21.4% (9/42) |
| `actions_only` / `step_best` | 19.0% (8/42) |

**All four are above base**, against AdamW's 11.9% / 0.0% on the same tasks and harness.
`actions_only` recovers too, so this is not a property of the thought corpus — it is the
optimizer/fit regime. The question this note answers: **does that transfer off retail?**

## LR selection — deliberately deferred

The multi-domain LR is **not** chosen at plan time. Only the 1e-3 rollouts existed when
this was written; the 3e-3 rollouts were still running. Picking "the better LR" before
that comparison landed would have been a guess. The chain reads the LR from
`/tmp/aprm/chosen_lr.txt`, written once the retail 3e-3 vs 1e-3 rollout comparison is in;
if the file is absent the chain defaults to 1e-3 and logs that loudly.

**DECISION (2026-09-02 03:55): LR = 1e-3.** Written to `/tmp/aprm/chosen_lr.txt`.

Rationale — the retail 3e-3 rollout came in far *below* 1e-3, and below base:

| retail, 42 tasks | eval action-PPL | completion |
|---|---|---|
| base (no Stage-2) | 3.8428 | 14.3% |
| **SGD 1e-3 `step_0020`** | **3.0847** | **31.0%** |
| SGD 1e-3 `step_best` (b199) | 2.4625 | 23.8% |
| SGD **3e-3** `step_best` (b199) | 2.2822 | **9.5%** |
| AdamW 1e-3 `step_0020` | 1.7828 | 11.9% |
| AdamW 1e-3 `step_best` | 1.7150 | 0.0% |

3e-3 fits *better* (2.2822 vs 2.4625) and behaves *worse* (9.5% vs 23.8%). Completion
peaks at a very light fit — PPL 3.08, barely below the untrained 3.84 — and declines
monotonically as fit deepens.

**This also resolves (A) vs (B) against (B).** SGD 3e-3 at PPL 2.28 scores 9.5%, *worse*
than AdamW 1e-3 `step_0020` at PPL 1.78 (11.9%), despite fitting less far. So SGD's sparse
update shape is **not** intrinsically protective; the earlier SGD wins came from
under-fitting, exactly reading **(A)**. The optimizer is only a means of controlling how
far the fit goes.

**Implication for this sweep:** `step_0020` is likely the best of the two snapshots in
every domain, and the true optimum may be *even earlier* than b20. `save_every 10` keeps
step_0010 onward, so earlier snapshots can be rolled out retrospectively without
retraining if the b20 numbers look like they are already past the peak.

## Design

Identical to the retail SGD runs so results are directly comparable — `sft_flat`,
corpus-wide + trajectory-wide shuffling, `steps_per_batch 32`, `num_batches 200`,
`eval_every 5`, `patience 6`, `save_every 10`, hide-observations, r8/a16,
`best_metric eval_actiononly_ppl`, `--optimizer sgd`. Only the domain changes.

**Arms (2):** `actions_only`, `thoughts_policy_adamw30`. The expert arms are dropped —
cc-12.0 showed the actions/thought contrast is the informative one and each extra arm
costs a full training run plus two rollouts.

**Corpora** (all `policy_adamw30`, verified 2026-09-02):

| domain | train / eval trajectories | source pool | mismatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| airline | 24 / 4 | `data/tau2_airline` | 0 |
| finance | 116 / 25 | `data/snorkel_finance_split_v3` (corrected) | 0 |
| insurance | 180 / 40 | `data/snorkel_insurance_split` | **14** |
| *(retail, done)* | 52 / 8 | `data/tau2_retail` | 0 |

**Rollouts.** 2 arms x {`step_0020`, `step_best`} per domain.

| domain | harness | status |
|---|---|---|
| airline | `run_sft_rollout_eval.sh` (tau2 gym) | proven |
| finance | `run_finance_rollout.sh`, **`MAX_TURNS=24`** | proven (at the env default of 12 every arm scored 0/10 — the policy never reached `respond_user`) |
| insurance | `snorkel_insurance_gym` | **UNPROVEN — smoke-test first** |

### Insurance is newly unblocked, but untested

cc-11.0 listed insurance rollouts as not runnable because the `rl_eval` uid -> task-index
map was an open TODO. That map now **exists and is complete**:
`data/splits/snorkel_insurance_uid_to_task.json` — `n_mapped=261, n_ambiguous=0,
n_missing=0`, with 40 `eval_task_ids` / 180 `train_task_ids`. The gym data is present
locally (`resources` 1.7M, `tool_data` 16K, `downsampled_task_set.json` 448K), so the
GitHub-clone blocker does not apply.

**But `logs/act_prm_snorkel_insurance_gym/` is empty — the gym has never produced a single
rollout.** So a 1-task smoke test runs before any full insurance pass. If it fails,
insurance still yields training results and only its rollout is skipped (the cc-11.0
posture), and the failure is recorded here rather than silently dropped.

## Cost

Training is ~1.6 h/run (6400 optimiser steps, roughly domain-independent) => ~10 h for 6
runs. Rollouts scale with task count: airline 18, finance 25, insurance 40 tasks =>
~35 / 50 / 80 min each, 12 rollouts => ~11 h. **Total ~21 h**, queued behind cc-12.0's
remaining ~12.5 h (3e-3 rollouts, nb1000 training, nb1000 rollout).

## Known risks going in

1. **Airline is tiny** — 24 train trajectories. 200x32 = 6400 steps is many epochs. SGD
   under-fits by design so this is less dangerous than it would be under AdamW, but the
   eval set is only **4 trajectories**, so airline's eval PPL is extremely noisy and
   `step_best` is close to meaningless there. Weight the 18-task rollout, not the PPL.
2. **Insurance corpus has 14 mismatches** of 220. Not blocking; noted so it is not
   mistaken for a clean export later.
3. **Insurance rollout may simply not work** (see above).
4. **n is small everywhere.** Airline 18 tasks and finance 25 give Wilson CIs wider than
   retail's 42. Expect to be able to detect "collapsed vs not", not 5-point differences.
5. **The retail result itself is not significant vs base** (31.0% vs 14.3%, Fisher
   p=0.116). Multi-domain replication is partly *how* that gets settled — consistent
   direction across four domains is stronger evidence than any single domain's n=42.

## Side-thread — old vs new SGD 3e-3, and the corpus ablation it motivated

**CORRECTION to a comparison I had been making.** I repeatedly cited "the historical SGD
3e-3 checkpoint scored 20.5-21.4%" as the anchor the new 3e-3 run underperformed. That
anchor was the wrong arm: 21.4% was `thoughts_policy_lr3e_3` **and** `expert_thoughts_all_lr3e_3`
(whose corrected-eval rerun scored only 14.3%). Like-for-like on retail, 42 tasks:

| arm | OLD (`sft`, nb150) | NEW (`sft_flat`, nb200) | Fisher p |
|---|---|---|---|
| `actions_only` | 11.9% (5/42) | **16.7%** (7/42) — *better* | 0.756 |
| `thoughts_policy` | 21.4% (9/42) | 9.5% (4/42) — worse | 0.227 |

**Neither is significant.** One arm rose, one fell. "The new 3e-3 is worse" is not
supported by this data.

**But the confound is asymmetric, and that is the interesting part.** `actions_only`
trains on expert actions and is **corpus-independent**, so for it old-vs-new isolates the
trainer/schedule change — and it went *up*. `thoughts_policy` changed **both**:

```
OLD: dataset_path=.../tau2_retail/policy           trainer=sft
NEW: dataset_path=.../tau2_retail/policy_adamw30   trainer=sft_flat
```

So the thought-arm drop may be a **Stage-1** effect (the `adamw30` relabel producing worse
thoughts) that the actions arm cannot reveal.

**Ablation queued** (`scripts/chain_corpus_ablation.sh`, last in the queue so it delays
nothing): retail `thoughts_policy` on the **old** `policy` corpus, with the current Stage-2
setup held fixed (`sft_flat`, SGD 1e-3, nb200, spb32). Compare against
`thoughts_policy_adamw30` SGD 1e-3 (`step_0020` 31.0%, `step_best` 23.8%) — same trainer,
optimizer, LR and tasks; only the Stage-1 corpus differs.

**Caveat on that ablation:** the old corpus has **80 train trajectories vs `adamw30`'s
52**, so "worse thoughts" and "more data" are confounded. Gradient-step count is identical
(spb32 x nb200), so the difference is data diversity / epochs, not optimisation budget.
A significant result would justify a matched-size follow-up, not an immediate conclusion.

## Results — COMPLETE (2026-09-03 00:55)

### Stage-2 training, SGD 1e-3 (best eval action-PPL; all cap-limited at b199, none plateaued)

| domain | `actions_only` | `thoughts_policy` | thoughts help? |
|---|---|---|---|
| retail | 2.7517 | **2.4625** | yes (-0.29) |
| airline | 2.8307 | **2.5375** | yes (-0.29) |
| finance | **2.0637** | 2.0800 | tied |
| insurance | **2.8049** | 2.9952 | no (+0.19) |

Scored spans verified byte-identical across arms within each domain (retail 13,459 action
tokens, airline 6,393, finance 34,497, insurance 35,663), so the arms are directly
comparable. The thought arm spends 24-30% of its gradient on thought tokens the metric
never scores, and still wins on both tau2 domains.

### Rollouts — the central result

`step_0020` vs `step_best`, same arm, same corpus, only the snapshot differs:

| domain | arm | `step_0020` | `step_best` | delta | Fisher p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| retail | `thoughts_policy` | 31.0% (13/42) | 23.8% (10/42) | -7.1 | 0.625 |
| retail | `actions_only` | 21.4% (9/42) | 19.0% (8/42) | -2.4 | 1.000 |
| airline | `thoughts_policy` | 61.1% (11/18) | 44.4% (8/18) | -16.7 | 0.505 |
| airline | `actions_only` | 55.6% (10/18) | 61.1% (11/18) | **+5.6** | 1.000 |
| **insurance** | `thoughts_policy` | 52.5% (21/40) | **15.0%** (6/40) | **-37.5** | **0.00076** |
| **insurance** | `actions_only` | 50.0% (20/40) | **10.0%** (4/40) | **-40.0** | **0.00017** |
| finance | `thoughts_policy` | 0.0% (0/10) | 0.0% (0/10) | 0.0 | 1.000 |
| finance | `actions_only` | 10.0% (1/10) | 0.0% (0/10) | -10.0 | 1.000 |

**POOLED (retail + airline + insurance, both arms): 42.0% (84/200) -> 23.5% (47/200),
Fisher p = 1.17e-04.**

7 of 8 arm-domain pairs decline. The single exception is airline `actions_only` at +5.6,
which is a **one-task** difference (10 vs 11 of 18) — within noise for that n.

### What this establishes

1. **Continuing Stage-2 SFT past a very early point destroys agentic capability while the
   SFT objective keeps improving.** This is now significant at the pooled level and, on
   insurance alone, at p<0.001 with n=40 per cell. Retail and airline pointed the same way
   but individually could not clear noise; insurance settles it.
2. **It is not about thoughts.** `actions_only` collapses on insurance just as hard as
   `thoughts_policy` (50.0->10.0 vs 52.5->15.0). Whatever is being destroyed is destroyed
   by fitting the action distribution, not by the thought corpus.
3. **`step_best` (PPL-optimal) is the wrong checkpoint**, confirmed across domains. Any
   selection criterion based on the SFT objective picks the worse policy.
4. Combined with cc-12.0's retail curve (completion peaks at PPL 3.08, monotone decline to
   0% at PPL 1.72) and the SGD-3e-3-vs-AdamW comparison, the variable is **fit depth**, not
   the optimizer.

### Caveats

- **Finance is uninformative** (floor effect, see challenge #6) — excluded from the pooled
  test. 9 of 10 finance fair-set rollouts ever run scored 0/10; the `hard` set is 0/29 for
  every arm in both generations.
- **No base reference for insurance yet**, so 52.5% is not interpretable in absolute terms
  (retail base 14.3%, airline base 55.6%; insurance has none — the gym was only unblocked
  today). `scripts/run_insurance_base_rollout.sh` is queued. The *within-insurance*
  comparisons above do not depend on it.
- Airline n=18 gives CIs of roughly +/-20 points; it can detect collapse, not 5-point moves.
- Insurance's thought corpus has 14 mismatches and its thought arm has worse action-PPL
  than `actions_only`, so it is the least clean domain — but the effect appears in *both*
  its arms, which is what makes the fit-depth reading robust rather than corpus-specific.

### Note on the smoke result itself

The smoke run scored `final_reward=-1.0` (eval) with `timesteps=0` on the base model at
`max_turns 10`. That is a **harness** pass, not a policy result: it proves the env
constructs, tools respond, the judge grades and a row is written. `-1.0` is the env's
clamped zero (`negative_rewards=True`), and 10 turns is well under the expert p50 of 12,
so a failure was the expected outcome. Do not read anything about insurance difficulty
from it.
