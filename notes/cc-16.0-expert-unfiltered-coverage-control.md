# cc-16.0 — `expert_thoughts` (unfiltered): isolating the turn-coverage variable

Started 2026-09-23. Machine idle, 1×H100 (97871 MiB free), no jobs running.

## Question

Why does `expert_thoughts_all` win every offline metric and convert none of it into
rollout success?

Offline it is the best arm in all four domains — insurance PPL 2.5542 vs 2.9952 for
`thoughts_policy` (a 0.44-nat edge, the largest of any arm), action-token accuracy
79.35% vs 76.10%. Online it is at the bottom of a cluster that is statistically
indistinguishable: insurance 97/200 = 48.5% vs `actions_only` 52.9% (p = 0.36), retail
27/126 = 21.4% vs `thoughts_policy` 29.8% (p = 0.11), airline 38/54 = 70.4% vs
`actions_only` 53.7% (p = 0.07, the *other* direction). Every pairwise test n.s.

So the thing to explain is the **absence of a gain**, not a deficit.

## What the investigation found (before any GPU time)

### 1. `--require_thought` drops turns; it does not add thoughts

Read `sft_flat.py:107-124`. The flag filters **targets** on the train split only (the
turns stay in context, so trajectories remain coherent). Measured trainable assistant
turns:

| domain | `expert_thoughts_all` | every other arm |
|---|---|---|
| insurance | 1458 / 2273 (64.1%) | 2273 (100%) |
| retail | 317 / 635 (49.9%) | 664 (100%) |
| airline | 119 / 221 (53.8%) | 239 (100%) |

And it is a **biased** subset — the turns where the expert chose to deliberate, i.e. the
hard ones. Confirmed independently: `eval_actiononly_ppl_ownthoughtsub` is *higher* than
overall eval PPL in all three domains (retail 2.2500 vs 2.1192, airline 2.4168 vs
2.3673, insurance 1.7756 vs 1.6817). Thought presence is confounded with turn difficulty.

Consequence: `expert_thoughts_all` differs from the EM arms in **three** ways at once —
thought source (GPT-5-class vs self-sampled), thought length (2-5× longer median, tails
to 3-4k chars vs a ~550-685 ceiling), and turn coverage. The PPL/rollout decoupling is
not attributable to any one of them.

### 2. **No arm actually thinks at rollout** (the bigger finding, free)

Wrote `scripts/measure_thought_emission.py` over the already-exported rollout text. It
counts, per (domain, arm, snapshot), the fraction of tool-call turns carrying ≥10 chars
of prose before `<tool_call>` — the same predicate `--require_thought` uses, so it is
directly comparable to corpus coverage.

Every thought arm is trained at **100%** coverage. At rollout, `step_0020`:

| domain | actions_only | thoughts_policy | thoughts_base | expert_thoughts_all |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| insurance | **16.7%** | 10.3% | 15.1% | 13.8% |
| retail | 3.8% | 4.5% | 4.9% | **5.9%** |
| airline | 6.5% | **8.9%** | 3.0% | 4.9% |
| finance | 73.0% | **95.0%** | — | 93.8% |

`actions_only`, trained with **zero** thoughts, emits them at the same rate or *higher*
than the thought-trained arms on insurance and retail. Stage-2 thought training barely
expresses itself at inference — which is a sufficient explanation for why corpus choice
does not move rollout success, and it subsumes the original question.

Two further patterns: `step_best` collapses emission to ~0-3% almost everywhere
(insurance `thoughts_policy` 0.2%, `actions_only` 0.7%) — moving *with* the known
`step_best` rollout collapse; and **finance is the exception at 73-95%**, the one domain
where thought training visibly took.

Verified against raw text before trusting it: on one insurance
`expert_thoughts_all` seed, 260 of 332 tool-call turns are a bare `<tool_call>` block
with nothing before it (21.7% with prose on that seed; 13.8% pooled). Not a parsing
artifact.

## The experiment now running

`scripts/chain_expert_unfiltered.sh` — train `expert_thoughts` (same pool, same expert
thoughts, **no** `--require_thought`) at SGD 1e-3 / nb200 / `sft_flat` / hide-obs, then
roll out `step_0020`. Moves turn coverage alone: verified with `SFT_DRY_RUN=1` that the
resolved command differs from the `expert_thoughts_all` run by exactly that one flag plus
the run tag, in all four domains.

Order — insurance first. It is the only domain whose noise floor (seed sd ~3.0pt, n=40)
resolves the ~5pt effects at issue; retail sd is 9.0pt, airline 22pt, finance n=10 has no
information. If the GPU is reclaimed, the informative half is already banked.

| phase | work | est |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | insurance SFT → 5 seed rollouts (1234/777/555/999/42) | 2.6h + 4.3h |
| 2 | retail + airline SFT → 3 repeats (one driver call covers both) | 3.7h + 5.4h |
| 3 | finance SFT → 1 rollout (fair set) | 1.9h + 0.4h |
| 4 | re-export all CSVs + thought-emission | minutes |

≈ 18h wall-clock, sequential on one GPU.

**Prediction, recorded before the fact:** this arm collapses to not thinking and lands
at or below `actions_only`. `train_sft.sh:84-86` already records that the unfiltered arm
"taught it not to think: at rollout it reasons before 0-8% of its tool calls" — that is
*why* `--require_thought` exists. It is still worth running because that prior was
measured on the old AdamW / SGD-3e-3 generations at `step_best` only, never at
`step_0020` on the generation the headline numbers come from; and because finding (2)
suggests trained coverage may not control rollout emission at all, in which case the
unfiltered arm will emit thoughts at the same 4-17% as everything else and the prior
will not replicate.

## Challenges hit and how they were resolved

- **My first hypothesis was wrong.** I claimed `expert_thoughts_all` trains on a bimodal
  target (half the turns thoughtless). The user pushed back — correctly — that
  `require_thought` was supposed to handle that. Reading `sft_flat.py:114` showed the
  training signal *is* 100% thought-bearing; the flag achieves that by deletion. The
  bimodality claim is true of the **unfiltered** `expert_thoughts` arm, not this one.
- **`train_sft.sh` defaults to `--trainer_config sft`**, not `sft_flat` (line 113,
  `${TRAINER_CFG:-sft}`). A plain invocation would have silently trained the wrong
  trainer. Caught by diffing the dry-run against the archived `expert_thoughts_all`
  command line; fixed with `TRAINER_CFG=sft_flat`.
- **The derived pool path is wrong for retail and finance.** `expert_thoughts` defaults
  to `data/<envname>_expert_thoughts`, but the shipped arms used
  `data/tau2_retail_expert_thoughts_matched` and
  `data/snorkel_finance_split_expert_thoughts_all_v3`, whose eval sets differ from the
  base — which silently makes the arm non-comparable (`train_sft.sh:88-91`). Pinned
  `EXPERT_POOL` per domain to the exact path each `expert_thoughts_all` run used.
- **`export_rollout_results.py` had no `sgdlr1e_3_rep1` key**, so the first tau2 repeat
  would have exported with `optimizer=unknown`. Added.
- Seeds: insurance rollouts call `main_pytorch.py` directly because
  `run_insurance_rollout.sh` has no `EXTRA_ARGS` passthrough and would silently drop
  `--seed`. tau2 repeats need no seed (unseeded external user sim) but do need distinct
  `CKPT_TAG`s so `.done` markers and log dirs don't collide.

## 2026-09-23 (later) — two findings from inspecting the SFT samples

### A. Thought emission is a per-episode MODE, and PPL is blind to it

Not a per-turn rate. Insurance, pooled over the 4 main arms:

| | step_0020 | step_best |
|---|---|---|
| P(thought \| prev turn had one) | **99.9%** (n=855) | **100.0%** (n=29) |
| P(thought \| prev turn had none) | 1.6% (n=6213) | 0.4% (n=7277) |
| thought rate at **turn 1** | 18.9% | **0.3%** |
| thoughts per episode (`thoughts_policy`) | 1.04 | **0.05** |

Thinking is absorbing: once entered, the model thinks on every remaining turn. Deeper
fitting does not reduce per-turn thinking — it reduces the probability of ENTERING
thinking mode, 18.9% -> 0.3% at turn 1. Absolute thoughts/episode drops 20x, so this is
not dilution by the step_best tool-call loop (9.5 -> 24.0 calls/ep).

Ruled out as causes:
- **Context mismatch.** Train (`data.py:282`) indexes the observation subsequence; rollout
  (`base.py:170`) indexes all messages. Different code, identical output at the configured
  `first=1,last=1` with alternating roles. (Latent fragility: they diverge at any other
  setting.)
- **Chat template.** Both paths `enable_thinking=False`; training render is byte-identical
  to the rollout prompt + target. No `<think>` prefill.
- **System prompt.** Corpus vs gym similarity 0.999.
- **Under-trained thought span.** Decomposed eval loss: action PPL 4.6953 -> 2.9952
  (-36.2%), **thought PPL 5.9740 -> 2.5321 (-57.6%)**. The thought span improves *more*.

Leading explanation (NOT verified): "think or not" is a single-token branch at the
assistant boundary, ~1.2% of an ~80-token target, so eval PPL is structurally blind to it.
The model learns continuation (99.9%) while entry decays. Why entry decays when 100% of
targets start with prose is unexplained.

**Decisive test, queued:** one forward pass per eval state on each saved checkpoint
(step_0010..0200), reading p(first target token is prose) vs `<`. Turns the inference into
a direct measurement and shows whether the decay is smooth or a phase change.

### B. The insurance EM thought corpus is 78% few-shot boilerplate — a real bug

`prompts.py:17-33` hard-codes ONE few-shot for the action->thought reversal, and it is a
**finance/`acme`** example ("What was the company's total revenue growth in 2024?", 
`get_descriptions(company_name="acme")`). It is used for every domain.

| domain | corpus | n | few-shot vocab | verbatim `acme` opener | dup prefix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| insurance | EM policy | 2273 | **77.7%** | 96 | 24.9% |
| insurance | EM base | 2273 | **74.2%** | 308 | 57.5% |
| insurance | expert | 1458 | 0.0% | 0 | 3.8% |
| retail | EM policy | 664 | 0.0% | 0 | 12.8% |
| airline | EM policy | 239 | 0.0% | 0 | 3.8% |
| finance | EM policy | 1347 | 5.2% | 2 | 5.9% |

Example, step t=1 of insurance trajectory 0 — an appetite question about a Maine B&B:
> *"I need to find revenue growth in 2024. Looking at revenue growth data could involve
> checking a revenue growth table."* → `get_table_data_dictionary(small_business_insurance_appetite)`

The thought contradicts the action it precedes. Contamination is insurance-only, almost
certainly because the insurance user turn ("Here is the company information: ... Question:")
most resembles the few-shot's format.

**What this invalidates.** Insurance is the ONLY domain with the power to resolve ~5pt
effects, and its EM corpora are mostly boilerplate — so "corpus choice is irrelevant"
was measured on a broken corpus there. Retail/airline EM corpora are clean but their
noise floors (9pt / 22pt) cannot resolve the difference. **We therefore have no clean
test of whether Act-PRM thoughts help.** That is a significant revision to the headline.

**What it does NOT explain.** The emission collapse happens for `actions_only` too
(16.7% -> 0.7%), which has no thoughts at all, so finding B does not cause finding A.

**Fix:** domain-appropriate few-shot (or none), plus a guard that rejects a sampled
thought naming entities absent from the state. Then regenerate the insurance Stage-1
corpora and re-run Stage-2. The running `expert_thoughts` chain is unaffected — the
expert corpora are 0.0% contaminated.

### C. Fix shipped — per-domain few-shots (2026-09-23)

The seed was never a prompt leak: it lives only in `build_thought_prompt_messages`
(`prompts.py:52`), the Stage-1 reversal prompt. `build_scoring_messages` (which computes
`p(x|s,z)`) and Stage-2 SFT both start from the task's own system prompt. The poisoning
is in the *generated thought text*, which then becomes the SFT target. `prompts.py:61`
also prepends "Great! Now do the same for the next task:" — an explicit imitate cue.

Structural test (n-gram overlap with the seed thought, not just finance vocabulary):

| domain | corpus | n | starts "I need" | shared 5-gram | shared 4-gram | "First I should" |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| insurance | EM base | 2273 | 81.7% | **15.3%** | **18.8%** | **8.4%** |
| insurance | EM policy | 2273 | 50.2% | 4.8% | 5.3% | 3.3% |
| retail | EM policy | 664 | 77.9% | **0.0%** | **0.0%** | **0.0%** |
| airline | EM policy | 239 | 78.7% | **0.0%** | **0.0%** | **0.0%** |
| finance | EM policy | 1347 | 68.1% | 0.7% | 0.8% | 0.5% |

**Style transferred everywhere** (the "I need ..." opener is 78-82% in all domains — the
few-shot teaching format, which is its job). **Content only transferred to insurance.**
Retail/airline samples are properly grounded: `chen_johnson_4204`, item `47602`,
"round-trip from LAS to IAH via PHX", "May 19, 2024".

**Why insurance and not retail/airline — task MODALITY, not prompt format** (correcting my
earlier guess). The seed is a SQL/table-exploration task (`get_descriptions`, "see which
tables exist"). Insurance is the same modality — `list_tables`, `get_table_descriptions`,
`get_table_schema`, `read_query` — so the seed's content is superficially applicable and
gets reused. Retail/airline are CRUD/API domains (`get_order_details`,
`book_reservation`) with a disjoint tool vocabulary: nothing to copy, so the model must
ground. Finance is same-modality AND same-subject, so its overlap is legitimate (0.7%
structural). Poisoning needs **same modality + different subject** — insurance is the only
cell that satisfies both.

Implemented:
- `prompts.py` — `FEWSHOT_{FINANCE,INSURANCE,RETAIL,AIRLINE}`, a `FEWSHOTS` registry, and
  `resolve_fewshot(domain)` (longest-key substring match; falls back to the finance seed so
  an unrecognised domain reproduces old behaviour rather than losing its example). Each
  example uses its own domain's real tool names and grounds the thought in entities only
  that episode could supply. `FEWSHOT` kept as an alias for back-compat.
- `build_thought_prompt_messages(..., fewshot=None)`.
- `base.py` — `fewshot_domain: str | None = None`; resolves from `cfg["env_config"]` when
  unset and logs which example it picked.
- `configs/generator/act_prm{,_actions_only}.yaml` — `fewshot_domain: null` (the key must
  exist or `update_configs` silently drops the CLI flag).
- `args.py` — `--fewshot_domain`.

Verified: `act_prm/snorkel_insurance{,_gym}` -> `list_tables`; `tau2_retail` ->
`find_user_id_by_email`; `tau2_airline` -> `get_reservation_details`;
`snorkel_finance_split` -> `get_descriptions`; unknown/None -> finance. All three touched
files compile; the running chain is unaffected (it is Stage-2 SFT on expert corpora, which
never touch the reversal prompt).

**Not yet done:** regenerating the insurance Stage-1 corpora with the fixed seed, and the
Stage-2 + rollout re-run needed to tell whether Act-PRM thoughts help on the one domain
that can measure it. Telecom/banking would fall back to the finance seed — harmless
(CRUD modality) but they should get their own example if they are ever revived.
